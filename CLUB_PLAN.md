## 1. Mission & positioning

Canadian Fish at Berkeley teaches, plays, and competes at Literature ("Canadian Fish"), a
six-player, two-team, pure-deduction card game — no bidding, no bluff-free-riding on luck: every
ask is public information and every book is won by reconstructing hidden hands from the log
([RULES.md](RULES.md) §1, §6). We run a free, zero-commitment weekly drop-in night with a
structured ladder inside it, a teaching pipeline for brand-new players, and duplicate-deal
tournaments that score the game fairly. Our own software is the club's infrastructure: `/learn`
for onboarding, bots for solo reps, `/practice` drills for deduction training, and live rooms as
a qualifier/overflow platform (README.md).

Our competitive claim is deliberately narrow and defensible: **to our knowledge, this would be
the first documented organized Literature circuit.** A 2026-08-20 survey found no documented
organized Literature/Fish tournament anywhere — US, India, Canada, or collegiate; the game's
reference pages describe no tournament structures at all
([pagat.com Literature](https://www.pagat.com/quartet/literature.html);
[Wikipedia — Literature (card game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literature_(card_game))), and a
search-engine AI summary claiming decades-old annual tournaments at Queen's/UofT/IIT was checked
against the candidate pages and found unsupported — treat that claim as fabricated
(research-clubs-tournaments.md §B5). We therefore say "first **documented**" — never "first ever" —
and the prior-art survey likewise found **no competitive infrastructure (ratings, ladders,
leagues, tournaments) in any digital implementation** (research-priorart.md, market gap #5),
so the circuit plus the app is genuinely unclaimed ground.

## 3. Meeting cadence & format

**Slot: one fixed weekday evening, 2 hours, every week — proposed Wednesday 7–9 PM.** A fixed
never-canceled weekly slot is the single most consistent trait of healthy campus game clubs
(research-clubs-tournaments.md §A2): Board Games at Berkeley holds Saturday 4 PM in Eshleman's
Bay View Room as a pure drop-in ([BGB CalLink][BGB] · [bgb.studentorg.berkeley.edu][BGB2]); MIT
Strategic Games Society runs Fri 6:05 + Sun 3:05 drop-ins ([mit.edu/~sgs][SGS]); Michigan Games
and Cards runs Tue + Fri 6 PM with "Tournament Tuesdays" as a light competitive overlay
([Maize Pages][MICH]); UW Chess Club's Tue 3:30–5 meeting mixes a **structured segment** (weekly
puzzle training, 5-minute blitz tourneys) into a casual night ([UW Chess][UWCHESS]). We copy the
structured-segment-inside-drop-in template exactly. We deliberately avoid Saturday 4 PM so we
never compete with BGB — we collaborate with them instead (EVENTS.md, event 8).

**The 2 hours:**

| Segment | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Teach | 0:00–0:20 | 20-minute lesson: week 1 = rules via `/learn` on phones + one guided hand vs Easy bots; later weeks = one `/strategy` topic + a `/practice` drill done together (app facts: README.md). Newcomers are **guaranteed a seat** in the first game after the teach (MIT/DL Bridge finds a partner for every walk-in — [MIT/DL Bridge][MITDL]). |
| Open tables | 0:20–2:00 | Casual 6-seat tables, bots filling short seats via the app; zero commitment, no dues (BGB model, [BGB]). |
| Ladder segment | 0:20–1:50 (parallel) | The structured segment: one Swiss-paired ladder match (2 games) for registered trios — full format F1 in [EVENTS.md](EVENTS.md) (UW Chess / Michigan Tournament-Tuesdays pattern, [UWCHESS] · [MICH]). |
| Close | 1:50–2:00 | Standings update, next-week teaser, room reset. |

**Rooms — the 20-hour workaround.** Weekday General Assignment classrooms under 399 capacity are
**free** for RSOs, but there is a **20 hour/semester weekday cap per org** — a 2-hour weekly
meeting across ~15 instructional weeks needs 30 h, so the cap forces a split ([EVENTSVC-CLASS]):

- ~10 weeks in a free weekday GA classroom (<399 cap; request 7 days ahead; spring requests open
  Dec 1; weeks 1–4 nothing before 8 PM) — [EVENTSVC-CLASS].
- ~5 weeks in **$0 RSO-rate ASUC Student Union rooms**: Eshleman conference rooms
  250/310/318/338/342/350 (~8–15 people), Eshleman Practice & Performance Rooms 10 (936 sqft) and
  240 (2,010 sqft), Bay View (720 sqft), MLK 82A/82D — rate chart is FY2023-24 and "subject to
  change" (**current rates UNVERIFIED**) — [EVENTSVC-RATES].
- **Blackouts**: no classroom reservations during RRR week or finals (fall: Dec 7–11, Dec 14–18;
  spring: May 3–7, May 10–14), holidays, or home-football gamedays; no RSO classroom use in
  summer ([EVENTSVC-CLASS] · [REG-CAL]). Last meeting of each semester is the week classes end
  (Dec 4 / Apr 30).

**Illustrative Spring 2027 slot plan** (booked in the Dec 1 request sprint; Wednesdays 7–9 PM;
instruction Jan 19 – Apr 30, spring recess Mar 22–26, per [REG-CAL]):

| Wednesdays | Weeks | Room | Hours vs 20 h cap | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jan 20, Jan 27, Feb 3, Feb 10 | 1–4 | Eshleman ($0 RSO rate: Bay View or PPR 240) | 0 h | Weeks 1–4: classrooms unreservable before 8 PM ([EVENTSVC-CLASS]); Eshleman sidesteps it ([EVENTSVC-RATES]) |
| Feb 17 – Mar 17, Mar 31 – Apr 28 (10 dates; Mar 24 skipped = recess) | 5–15 | Free weekday GA classroom <399 cap | 10 × 2 h = **20 h, exactly at the cap** | Free weekday rooms; 7-day lead already satisfied by Dec 1 block request ([EVENTSVC-CLASS]) |
| May 3–14 | RRR + finals | — none — | — | Classrooms unavailable RRR/finals ([EVENTSVC-CLASS]); Drill League only (EVENTS.md event 11) |

Fall 2026 has no reservable era until registration completes (~Nov 20): informal pickup
sessions + app rooms, then the Founders' Cup by Dec 4 (§2). Any home-football-gameday
collisions get swapped to an Eshleman room ([EVENTSVC-CLASS]).

Capacity math: one Fish table = 6 players; 10–40 attendees = 2–7 tables ≈ any 40-cap classroom
or Eshleman PPR 240. A 10–40-person card night is far below the Major Event Policy bar
([LEAD-events], major-event assessment is for large-scale events only).

## 4. Officer roles

OASIS requires exactly **four signatories** for a new org (UCB students/staff/faculty, ≥2
actively enrolled students; all four complete Signatory Training) — [LEAD-new]. We map the four
to real jobs; small boards work (MIT SGS runs on 7 officers total, [SGS]). New RSOs need **no
faculty/staff advisor** ([LEAD-new]).

**President (Signatory 1).** Owns the OASIS relationship end-to-end: application, orientation,
office hours, registration, constitution amendments filed with OASIS ([LEAD-new]). Owns the
CalLink page (created by OASIS at registration; used for events, photos, social links —
[LEAD-callink]) and the ASUC sponsorship application ([ASUC-EVP]). Runs officer meetings and the
May transition/returning-org re-registration ([LEAD-return]).

**Treasurer / ASUC Finance Agent (Signatory 2).** Completes the annual **ASUC Finance Agent
Training** (finance agents must be registered students with current-year training) and runs all
money through CalLink's 5-stage requisition (Stage 2 ~5 business days; Stage 4 review for
stipends/contracts/>$2,500; payment runs every two weeks during semesters). RSOs **cannot open
outside bank accounts** — we operate under the ASUC nonprofit tax-ID ([SU-finance]). Files
Senate Contingency (year 1) and ABSA (spring, for 2027-28) ([LEAD-fund]).

**Tournament Director (Signatory 3).** Owns formats, deal generation/sealing, ladder records,
and results (procedures in [EVENTS.md](EVENTS.md)). Owns **room reservations** — only certified
signatories can reserve campus space ([LEAD-space]) — including the Dec 1 spring-request sprint
and the 20-hour-cap ledger ([EVENTSVC-CLASS]). Secures **prior OASIS approval for any
prize/games-of-chance event** and enforces the no-entry-fee/no-cash-prize line ([LEAD-events]).

**New-Player Lead (Signatory 4).** Owns the teach segment, the newcomer-seat guarantee, the
Discord (the real hub — BGB's portal is compliance, Discord is infrastructure, [BGB2]), and
recruitment: Cub-E tabling, flyers via approved channels, Calapalooza booth ([LEAD-promote] ·
[LEAD-cala]). Curates `/learn` + `/practice` onboarding paths in the app (README.md).

### Cash-flow mechanics (why the plan buys nothing it can't float)

All club money moves through CalLink's **5-stage requisition** under the ASUC nonprofit tax-ID —
RSOs cannot open outside bank accounts. Stage 2 takes ~5 business days; Stage 4 review applies
to stipends, contracts, and anything over $2,500; payment runs go out every two weeks during
semesters ([SU-finance]). Senate Contingency money lands ~2–3 weeks after Senate approval
([LEAD-fund]). Practical consequences:

1. Nothing in year 1 goes near the $2,500 Stage-4 review line.
2. Reimbursements are planned around the two-week payment cadence — no "buy it tonight for
   tomorrow" purchases through ASUC.
3. Until Contingency lands (earliest ~mid-Dec 2026 on our timeline), the club runs on the
   $0-room plan plus ~$45 of decks — the only unavoidable pre-funding cost.

Year 1 is deliberately fundable out of pocket if Contingency under-delivers: the whole plan
stands at ≈ $0 rooms + ~$400 of estimated hardware.

## 7. Retention

Copied from patterns the research attributes to specific healthy clubs
(research-clubs-tournaments.md §A2):

- **Sub-identities.** BGB's MTG and D&D sub-communities give members a persistent home inside
  the club ([BGB] · [BGB2]). Ours: ladder trios (named teams), drill-league solvers, TD/teaching
  crew.
- **Ladder standings as identity.** A semester-long published ladder (format F1) gives every
  week stakes and every trio a trajectory — the structured-segment pattern (UW Chess blitz
  nights [UWCHESS]; Michigan Tournament Tuesdays [MICH]).
- **One marquee food event per semester.** MIT SGS's monthly "Super Sunday" (noon–midnight,
  Morss Hall, free food) is the model ([SGS]); ours is the semester marathon + championship
  (EVENTS.md), with food per the EH&S pre-packaged path ([EHS-food]).
- **Newcomer-seat guarantee.** MIT/DL Bridge finds a partner for every walk-in ([MITDL]); our
  6-seat tables adopt a slot-in norm — a newcomer never waits out a full game (bots yield seats
  to humans in app-hosted tables; README.md).
- **Teaching-club identity.** Bridge Club at Berkeley self-describes as a "teaching club" and
  still wins nationals — casual Sunday teaching plus national competition (2026 Mitch Dunitz
  Open Team Championship at the Collegiate Bridge Bowl; NACBC titles 2014/2018/2019/2026)
  ([BRIDGE-CAL] · [ACBL-BOWL] · [NACBC]). Two-tier "teach weekly, compete at the top" is our
  explicit structure: everyone can learn; trios that want blood get the ladder and the
  championship.

**The semester rhythm** (every retention lever above, on a calendar; event mechanics in
[EVENTS.md](EVENTS.md)):

| When | Ritual |
|---|---|
| Every week | Weekly night: teach → open tables → ladder round; standings published same night |
| Every week (async) | Drill League set drops Monday, leaderboard locks Sunday |
| Mid-semester | Bot Gauntlet night (Gauntlet Wall inductions) + BGB collab exchange |
| Once/semester | Noon-to-midnight marathon (the marquee food event — SGS Super Sunday model, [SGS]) |
| Semester end | Championship (F2), then the pre-RRR decompression night with awards — always before classes end (Dec 4 / Apr 30, [REG-CAL]) |
| Rivalry week | Big Game week themed night; long-term: the Stanford varsity match (EVENTS.md Part 3) |
